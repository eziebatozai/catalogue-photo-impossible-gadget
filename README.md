A studio catalogue photograph of the **FrostToast**: a brushed-chrome
two-slot toaster that toasts ice cubes. Two clear ice cubes pop out of
the slots, frost dusting the chrome, a single small glass of water
standing beside it as the one accessory. Shot like a real manufactured
product on a seamless pale-grey background — the absurd job reads at a
glance, no caption needed.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) copies the
baked-in photograph `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
